Bio

Ismail Hafiane is a 23-year-old NOC Analyst based in Morocco. Driven by a deep curiosity for how networks and systems operate under the hood, he is currently building his expertise in infrastructure monitoring and incident management at Zen Networks Inc., working across AWS, OCI, and Outscale.

Beyond his daily shift, Ismail is a strong believer in continuous learning and the open-source mindset. He spends his free time breaking and fixing things in his homelab, preparing for his RHCSA, and automating repetitive tasks with Bash.

His ultimate vision? To evolve from NOC and SysAdmin roles into a fully-fledged Cloud Security Engineer, building infrastructure that is both resilient and secure.
